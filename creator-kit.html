The app in one sentence

Feed Flow! is a calm baby feeding app for iPhone that keeps breastfeeding, bottles, pumping, formula, water and solids in one place, with quick one-handed logging and a clear view of the day.

It was made by Robin, a dad of two in Norway, after a messy combo feeding start with our first. It took about three years to build, and every version was tested with our own kids.

What parents tend to like

  • Everything in one timeline. Nursing, pumping, bottles, formula and solids together, so mixed feeding is easy to see.
  • Calm, one-handed logging. Made for tired 3 am moments: a slider and one smart button, no forms.
  • Bottle timer. A prepared-bottle countdown that can stay on the lock screen.
  • FlowCast. A gentle guess at when the next feed may be due, based on the baby's own history.
  • First foods and allergens. Track new foods, the common allergens and any reactions.
  • A report to share. A PDF parents can bring to their pediatrician, health visitor or lactation consultant.

How we like to work together

Please do

  • Try the app properly first, with real feeds.
  • Say what you honestly think, in your own words. There is no script.
  • Mark it as an ad or paid partnership when it is one, and say so when the app was a gift.
  • Tag us and link to the App Store or tryfeedflow.app (or your own code, if you have one).

Please don't

  • Make medical claims. Feed Flow! keeps records, it doesn't give medical advice or diagnoses.
  • Shame any way of feeding. Breast, bottle, formula, combo: all welcome.
  • Promise money savings or results for every baby.
