Shared feeding log

Which baby tracker can my partner and I both log in?

Feed Flow lets you invite everyone who looks after your baby to the same log. Each person logs from their own iPhone through their own iCloud, so nobody has to ask "did she eat?" again. Sharing is free and there is no limit on caregivers.

Download on the App Store

Free to log · iPhone · Your baby’s data stays in your own iCloud

How sharing works

Invite a partner, grandparent, nanny or sitter from the app. Once they accept, you both see and add to the same baby log.

  • Everyone uses their own phone. Each caregiver logs through their own iCloud. There is no shared password.
  • No caregiver limit. Add as many people as look after your baby.
  • The same countdown for everyone. A bottle one person makes shows its countdown for the next person too.
  • Free. Family sharing is part of free logging. Premium adds a detailed activity feed of who logged what.

Handovers without the questions

The night shift, the morning at grandma's, the sitter on date night: each handover usually comes with the same questions. When did she last eat? How much? Was that bottle just made? With one shared log the answer is already on everyone's phone.

Vitamin D drops, solids and allergen introductions are shared the same way, so nobody gives the drops twice.

Your baby's data stays yours

The baby log lives on your phones and in your own iCloud accounts, not on an advertising platform. Feed Flow has no ads. You decide who is invited.

Common questions

Can my partner and I both log feeds?
Yes. Invite your partner to the same baby log and both of you log from your own iPhone.
Is there a limit on how many caregivers I can add?
No. You can invite everyone who looks after your baby.
Does sharing cost extra?
No. Family sharing is free. Premium adds a detailed activity feed showing who logged what.
Does everyone need an iPhone?
Yes. Feed Flow is an iPhone app, and each caregiver logs through their own iCloud.
