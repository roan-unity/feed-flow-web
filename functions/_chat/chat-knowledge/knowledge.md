# Feed Flow! knowledge (facts only, condensed from the website: llms.txt, faq, support)

## What it is
Feed Flow! is an iPhone app for keeping feeding, growth and baby health records in one place. It is built in Norway by Zeolite. Download: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6782075454
It is a record-keeping app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend medicine doses, give medical or nutrition advice, or replace a pediatrician, pharmacist, midwife, health nurse or lactation professional.
It has no ads and no cross-app tracking.

## Logging feeds
- Press and hold the button on the home screen to pick a type (formula, breast, pumped, water or solids), slide to the amount, and tap to log. It works one-handed.
- You can start a live timer, adjust the start time before or during a feed, and edit or delete any feed later from the history.
- Today at a glance: bottles, breastfeeding, pumping, solids and water appear in one timeline. Bottle totals show in ml or oz.
- Breastfeeding and pumping: you can log nursing, pumping, expressed milk and formula together, without judgement.
- Breastfeeding amounts are honest estimates, because nobody can measure milk from the breast. Where nursing is part of the day, the app leans on feeds a day and rhythm, not a precise millilitre count.
- "Smart sizes": instead of asking you to set up bottle sizes, the app quietly learns the amounts you usually pour and offers them as one-tap buttons above the slider. No setup. You can turn it off in Settings.
- It works fully offline. When you are back online it syncs to your own iCloud so your own devices stay in step.

## Bottle countdown (safe window)
- Prepared formula and expressed milk are only good for a limited time. The app shows a calm countdown on each prepared bottle, so you can see at a glance whether it is still good and bin less by accident.
- A formula bottle gets a one-hour countdown by default after feeding starts. The countdown can also show on the lock screen.
- It is a memory aid, not a safety sensor.
- The bottle timers are free. Anything to do with safety is never paywalled.

## FlowCast (next feed)
- FlowCast learns the baby's recent rhythm and shows when the next feeds are likely due, as gentle windows, not exact promises.
- It needs about four days of feeds before it can show a likely next-feed time. When the pattern is shifting, it says so.
- It is a planning guide, not a schedule you have to hit and not a medical prediction.
- Premium adds a day-ahead view. Notifications are optional.
- Usual feed sizes can help families prepare closer to what is needed and waste less milk or formula.

## Is my baby eating enough?
- The app shows a typical range for the baby's age and weight as a gentle guide. It is never a grade or a diagnosis. Every baby is different, and a day outside the range is an invitation to look closer, not an alarm.
- For anything that worries a parent, they should follow their pediatrician or midwife.

## Wasting less
- The app does not promise that it will save money. Smart sizes, safe-window timers and analysis are tools that help waste less milk and formula. How much that is depends entirely on the family's feeding. It is shown as an honest estimate of milk not wasted, never as a saving owed.
- Some waste is normal. The app never judges. It only shows where a smaller bottle might help.

## Formula brands
- The app keeps product details and lets you compare label-listed energy, protein, fat, carbohydrates and vitamin D.
- Feed Flow does not rank or recommend brands.

## Growth and development
- Track weight, height and head circumference against WHO growth references. See percentile context and follow the baby's own measurements over time.
- Record developmental milestones with dates, notes and photos.
- Source for the WHO references: https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards

## Baby health
- Track nine major allergen groups, food attempts and later reaction notes.
- Log medicines and vaccination dates, without dosage or schedule advice.
- Log vitamin D drops and the vitamin D listed on the formula label.
- Keep health notes beside feeding and growth, for conversations with a pediatrician, health nurse or lactation professional.
- Feed Flow does not diagnose allergies, assess causation, calculate medicine doses or replace professional care.

## Sharing with family
- Up to five people can log to the same baby through iCloud, for example a partner or a grandparent. Everyone sees feeds as they happen. No extra account is needed. The log stays private to the family.
- Premium adds the detailed activity feed.

## Free and Premium
- Logging every feed is free, forever. The rule is: logging is free, the deeper understanding is Premium.
- Premium adds deeper planning (including the FlowCast day-ahead view), the activity feed, the vitamin D meter, full history, full growth curves and longer analytics, weekly trends, Baby Reports (the PDF report), and more than one child.
- A separate Reports Pass is also available. There is a free trial, and a one-time Lifetime option for people who would rather not subscribe.
- Current regional prices and purchase options are shown in the App Store. Do not quote amounts.
- Restore a purchase: Settings > Premium > Restore purchases. The unlock is tied to the Apple ID, so it restores free on any device signed in to the same account. If a purchase still does not appear, email support with the Apple ID region it was bought in.
- Manage or cancel a subscription: the iOS Settings app > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose Feed Flow!. Cancelling stops future renewals, and access continues until the current period ends. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews. Subscriptions are handled by Apple.

## Privacy and your data
- Feeding and growth data stays on the phone and in the parent's own iCloud. Feed Flow has no server that holds it and cannot read it. Website analytics are separate from baby records.
- Export: Settings > Data & Privacy > Export my data. It creates a complete, machine-readable copy of everything logged, plus a readable CSV, to save or share.
- Delete: Settings > Data & Privacy > Delete all data. It erases the data from the device and, because it lives in the parent's iCloud, from their synced devices too.
- Reinstalling: with iCloud sync on, data comes back when you reinstall and sign in to the same Apple ID. If the data was kept on the device only (iCloud off), reinstalling starts fresh.
- Privacy policy: https://tryfeedflow.app/privacy.html

## Settings people ask about
- Units: millilitres or ounces. The app picks the unit from the phone's region, and it can be changed under Units in the app's Settings. Amounts are stored once and only shown differently.
- Themes: the app has colour themes (Ocean, Forest, Lavender, Midnight) and a night mode. They are chosen under Appearance in the app's Settings.
- More than one baby, twins: add another baby in the app's Settings. Having more than one child in the app is part of Premium.
