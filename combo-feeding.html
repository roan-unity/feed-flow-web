Combo feeding tracker

Which app tracks breastfeeding and formula together?

Feed Flow was built for combo feeding. Nursing, pumping, expressed milk and formula go in one timeline, the day's milk total counts all of them, and everyone who feeds your baby logs into the same record. Logging is free.

One timeline instead of two apps

Combo feeding usually means two kinds of records that never meet: nursing times in one place, bottle amounts in another, pumping somewhere else. Feed Flow puts them in the same day.

  • Nursing: log a session in a few taps.
  • Pumping: log what you expressed, and see it next to what your baby actually took.
  • Bottles: formula or expressed milk, prepared and drunk amounts in ml or oz.
  • Solids and water: in the same timeline, shown separately from the milk total.

One milk total for the whole day

The day's milk total counts formula, pumped milk and nursing. Nursing is counted as an estimate, since nobody can see how much a baby drinks at the breast. You get one number for the day instead of adding up two lists at night.

No judgement either way

Combo feeding can be planned, temporary or just what works this week. Feed Flow treats breast milk and formula as parts of the same record, not as a score. It does not assess milk supply or tell you how much your baby should drink.

When more than one person feeds

Combo feeding often means a partner gives bottles while you nurse or pump. Invite them to the same baby log and each of you logs from your own phone. The bottle countdown on the Lock Screen covers every prepared bottle, whoever made it.

With Premium, FlowCast uses your baby's own history to suggest a likely window for the next feed after about four days of logging, and a Baby Report gives your health nurse or lactation consultant a clear summary.

Common questions

Can I track breastfeeding and formula in the same app?
Yes. Feed Flow logs nursing, pumping, expressed milk and formula in one timeline, with one daily milk total.
How does Feed Flow count nursing in the total?
Nursing is included as an estimate, alongside the measured amounts from formula and expressed milk bottles.
Can my partner log bottles while I log nursing?
Yes. Invite them to the same baby log and each of you logs from your own iPhone.
Is combo feeding tracking free?
Yes. Logging every kind of feed is free, with no ads. Premium adds FlowCast, the activity feed, full history, growth curves and Baby Reports.
