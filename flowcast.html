A calm next-feed notification, learned from your baby.

Next-feed predictions from your baby's own rhythm

After about four days of logged feeds FlowCast looks at your baby's recent pattern and shows a likely window for the next feed and the day ahead.

The estimate can include a likely amount and the gaps between feeds. It is built from your records, so it reflects this baby rather than a generic feeding timetable.

  • See the next likely feeding window and when it opens.
  • Review later windows across the day.
  • Use estimated amounts as preparation guidance, not a portion your baby must finish.
  • Watch the prediction become more useful as consistent history builds.

An optional notification when the next feed approaches

Once your baby's rhythm is steady, about a week of regular days, Feed Flow can send a quiet reminder before the next window. It stays silent overnight. That gives you time to warm milk, check a bottle or simply take a breath before the next feed.

The reminder is optional and follows the prediction. Feed Flow refreshes its timing as new feeds are logged and when you return to the app, helping it avoid relying on an old window.

A nudge, never an alarm you must obey

Feed when your baby needs it, whether that is before, during or after the predicted window. FlowCast does not assess hunger, diagnose a feeding issue or replace your judgement.

Smart meal planning with less waste

FlowCast works beside the app's learned bottle sizes. Together they help you answer two practical questions: when might the next feed happen, and roughly how much should we prepare?

Preparing closer to your baby's usual amount can reduce leftovers without pressuring anyone to finish. A little milk left over is normal, and an empty bottle is never the goal at any cost.

What FlowCast does not know

Growth spurts, illness, travel, daycare and ordinary chaotic days can all change a feeding pattern. FlowCast cannot see those causes and does not promise that the next feed will happen inside its window.

Use it as a gentle planning aid beside your baby's cues and your health professional's guidance. It is not a medical prediction or a feeding schedule to enforce.

Common questions

When does FlowCast start predicting the next feed?
After about four days of feeding history, FlowCast can begin showing likely windows from your baby's own logged rhythm. More consistent history gives it a clearer basis.
Can FlowCast notify me before the next feed?
Yes. Once your baby's rhythm is steady, about a week of regular days, Feed Flow can send a quiet reminder before the next window. It stays silent overnight. It is a planning nudge, not an alarm you need to follow.
Is FlowCast free?
Logging is free. The next-feed time and the day-ahead view are part of Premium. Current options are shown in the App Store.
Should I feed my baby only inside the FlowCast window?
No. Always respond to your baby's cues and professional guidance. A predicted window is a useful estimate, not a rule or a medical recommendation.
