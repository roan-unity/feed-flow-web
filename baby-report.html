Feeding history, growth curves and milestones in one PDF

A Baby Report brings the information you already logged into one calm document. Start with the baby's summary, then move into feeding patterns and growth rather than assembling screenshots and notes before an appointment.

  • Milk and feed totals, daily averages and the period covered.
  • Weekly feeding patterns and amounts prepared, taken and left.
  • Weight, height and head circumference with WHO reference percentiles.
  • Developmental milestones with their recorded dates.

The detailed growth page explains what a percentile means in plain language and keeps the emphasis on following your baby's own curve rather than chasing a score.

Made for a better professional conversation

Print the report, share the PDF or open it during a visit. A health nurse can see the trend, a pediatrician can review the broader picture, and an IBCLC can discuss feeding history with concrete amounts and times.

Feed Flow does not diagnose from the report or replace an examination. It gives you and your chosen professional a clearer starting point.

Private in your iCloud until you decide to share

Your baby's records live on your device and in your own iCloud, not on a Feed Flow server. Caregiver sharing uses your private iCloud setup. Exporting or sharing a report is an action you take, not something that happens automatically.

You can also export your underlying data or delete it. The report is a view of your record, not a separate advertising profile.

Common questions

What is included in the Baby Report?
Feeding totals and patterns, weekly trends, WHO growth curves and percentiles for weight, height and head circumference, and developmental milestones from your own records.
Can I share the report with my pediatrician or IBCLC?
Yes. You can print or share the PDF before or during an appointment. It is a conversation aid, not a diagnosis.
Is my baby's report stored on Feed Flow's servers?
No. Baby data stays on your device and in your own iCloud. You decide when to export or share a report.
Do I need full Premium for reports?
Reports are included with Premium, and a separate Reports Pass is available. The App Store shows current options and prices.
