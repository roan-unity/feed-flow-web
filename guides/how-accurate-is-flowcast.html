FlowCast predictions

How accurate is FlowCast?

Generally quite accurate. The longer answer comes down to two things: how complete your logging is, and how settled your baby's feeding pattern is. Here is what that means in practice.

Your logging is the source of truth

FlowCast learns only from the feeds you log. That is the whole basis for the prediction. A missed night feed, or a bottle logged hours later, looks to FlowCast like a different rhythm, so the windows drift.

The more complete the log, the closer the windows get. Logging at the time helps, and so does fixing the time afterwards when you forget. If someone else feeds the baby, family sharing lets them log in the same record, so nothing goes missing.

A settled pattern makes a sharper forecast

After about four days of history, FlowCast starts showing likely windows from your baby's own rhythm. How sharp they are depends on how steady that rhythm is.

Babies change their patterns a lot. There are transition periods where the gaps between feeds shift, growth spurts, and days when a baby is unwell or about to get sick. In those periods FlowCast is less sure. It still tries, weighting earlier patterns differently, but the windows are a rougher guide until things settle again.

When FlowCast is at its best

Once your baby has a fairly steady feeding pattern, FlowCast becomes a really nice tool for preparing meals and getting an idea of what to expect. You can see when the next feed is likely, plan your day around the windows, and with Premium see the likely amount and the whole day ahead.

What FlowCast cannot know

FlowCast cannot see why a pattern changes. It does not know about illness, teething, travel or a busy day at daycare, and it does not assess hunger. Feed when your baby needs it, whether that is before, during or after the window. It is a planning aid, not a schedule to follow.

What we are working on next

A baby's day has other rhythms too. We are working toward bringing FlowCast to sleep first, and later to diapers.

Common questions

How long does FlowCast need before it is accurate?
It starts showing likely windows after about four days of logged feeds. It gets sharper as your baby settles into a fairly steady pattern and your log stays complete.
Does FlowCast work for breastfed babies?
Yes. It learns from the feeds you log, whether bottles or nursing, so it follows your own baby's rhythm.
Is FlowCast free?
The next-feed guide on Home is free. Premium adds the fuller day-ahead forecast and deeper planning tools. Current options are shown in the App Store.
Should I feed only inside the predicted window?
No. Always respond to your baby's cues and your health professional's guidance. A predicted window is a useful estimate to plan around, not a rule.
