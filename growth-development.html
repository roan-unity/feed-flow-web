Baby weight tracking with WHO percentile context

A weight measurement on its own is hard to interpret. Feed Flow places weight in the context of age and sex using WHO growth references, then shows a percentile and the pattern created by your baby's recorded measurements. Log weight, height and head circumference and see them against the WHO band, with the percentile named.

The example above is a real app report: 7.4 kg at the 49th weight percentile and 66.3 cm at the 50th height percentile. The percentile is context, not a grade. A higher number is not a better baby, and a single measurement does not establish a growth problem.

Understand baby growth percentiles

Height and head circumference belong in the same record

Track weight, height and head circumference together instead of keeping separate notes from each check-up. Feed Flow compares the measurements with WHO growth references and keeps them beside your child's broader development history.

  • Weight-for-age: follow the recorded weight pattern and percentile.
  • Length or height: keep growth measurements together over time.
  • Head circumference: retain another important check-up measurement in context.
  • Premium growth curves: explore longer history and trends beyond everyday logging.

The World Health Organization publishes the underlying WHO Child Growth Standards. Your health professional may use a different national reference or interpret measurements alongside examination and history.

Development is more than a growth chart

Record developmental milestones with dates, notes and photos. Keep those moments next to feeding and health records so the story of a check-up does not begin and end with weight.

Feed Flow does not screen for developmental conditions or tell you whether a milestone is delayed. It helps you remember what happened and bring questions to your health nurse or pediatrician.

A report ready for the next conversation

Premium Baby Reports turn your own logged information into a clear PDF that can be printed or shared before a visit. A separate Reports Pass is also available in the App Store for families who want reports without full Premium.

Use the report to discuss a pattern, not to self-diagnose. Measurement technique, timing, prematurity, health history and clinical examination all matter.

When to ask for advice

Contact your pediatrician or local child health service if you are worried about feeding, growth, hydration, development or a change in your child's usual pattern. Do not wait for an app threshold to seek help.

Common questions

Does Feed Flow use WHO baby growth charts?
Yes. It places weight, height and head circumference measurements in the context of WHO growth references. Your clinician may also use local references and clinical assessment.
What does the 49th weight percentile mean?
In the reference population, about 49 percent of children of the same age and sex weigh less and about 51 percent weigh more. It is not a score or a target.
Can I track head circumference as well as weight and height?
Yes. Head circumference, weight and height can be kept in the same growth record with WHO reference context.
Are growth curves free?
Everyday milestone logging is free. Premium includes full growth curves, unlimited history and broader analytics; current options are shown in the App Store.
