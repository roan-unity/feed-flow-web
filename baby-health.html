Medicine records without dosing guesswork

Log when a medicine was given and keep the name and your own notes in the health timeline. This is especially useful when caregivers share responsibility or a clinician later asks what happened and when.

Feed Flow does not calculate doses, check interactions, recommend medicines or tell you when the next dose is safe. Use the medicine label and instructions from your pediatrician or pharmacist.

Vaccination dates in the same baby history

Record vaccines and their dates alongside other health events. Instead of searching through papers before an appointment, you can keep the vaccination note with the rest of your child's health record.

Vaccine schedules differ by country and individual circumstance. Feed Flow does not create a schedule or tell you which vaccine is due; use your national programme and health professional for that guidance.

Allergens, symptoms and health reactions over time

Food introductions and later observations belong on a timeline. Log egg yesterday, add a reaction note today, and retain both events with their actual times. The same record can hold other health notes you want to discuss.

Timing is not causation

A symptom recorded after a food or medicine does not prove that it caused the symptom. Feed Flow does not diagnose allergies or adverse effects. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek emergency care rather than stopping to log.

Vitamin D drops and formula-label contributions

Log vitamin D supplements and record the vitamin D listed on your formula label. Keeping both in one place makes it easier to discuss your child's actual routine with a health visitor, pharmacist or pediatrician.

Recommendations vary by feeding method, formula intake, age and country. Feed Flow adds your logged drops to the vitamin D in formula, read from the label values. It does not recommend a supplement or a dose.

Bring a coherent record to the appointment

Feeding, growth, medicines, vaccines and reactions can all matter in the same conversation. Feed Flow keeps them together so you can spend less time searching and more time explaining what you have observed.

Reports include feeding, growth, milestones, allergens and reactions. You choose what to share and with whom; your baby's data stays in your own iCloud.

Common questions

Can Feed Flow remind me which medicine dose to give?
No. It keeps a record of medicines and times but does not recommend or calculate doses. Follow the label and your clinician or pharmacist.
Can I log vaccines from my national programme?
Yes. Record the vaccination and date in your child's health history. Feed Flow does not create or replace an official vaccination schedule.
Does the app track vitamin D from both drops and formula?
Yes. It can log supplement drops and retain vitamin D information from the formula label so both appear in your records.
Can Feed Flow diagnose a reaction?
No. It records observations and timing for discussion with a professional. Seek urgent medical help for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.
