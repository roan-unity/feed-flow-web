For lactation and feeding professionals

Feed Flow is a record-keeping companion for families, not a clinical decision tool. IBCLCs, lactation experts, dietitians and feeding professionals can use parent-shared records to make conversations more concrete.

  • Review feeding sequences and bottle details rather than relying on recall.
  • Keep growth, health notes and reactions in context.
  • Share only information the parent chooses to provide.
  • Keep diagnosis, treatment and individualized feeding advice with the qualified professional.
