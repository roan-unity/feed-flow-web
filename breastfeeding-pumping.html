Breastfeeding, pumping & mixed feeding

Your feeding routine does not need separate apps.

Nurse, pump, give expressed milk, use formula, or combine them all. Feed Flow keeps the whole feeding picture together and turns it into useful history for you and the professionals supporting you.

A breastfeeding log that stays out of the way

Record nursing sessions in a few taps and see minutes at the breast beside bottle totals. The purpose is not to grade a feed or tell you how feeding should look. It is to give you a reliable memory of what happened when the days and nights start to blur.

  • Nursing, bottles, pumping, water and solids all appear in the day's timeline. The milk total counts formula, pumped milk and nursing (nursing as an estimate). Solids and water are shown separately.
  • See daily amounts and patterns without separating breast and bottle into different worlds.
  • Share the same baby log with a partner or another caregiver.
  • Keep private feeding notes with Premium when you want more context.

Pumping and expressed milk stay connected

Pumping is both its own task and part of a later feed. Feed Flow keeps pumping records and expressed-milk bottles in the same picture, making it easier to understand what was expressed, what was offered and what your baby actually took. Premium compares what you pump with what your baby takes.

You do not need to choose a single feeding identity in the app. A day can include nursing, pumping and formula, and each remains a valid part of the record.

No judgement, no supply promises

Feed Flow does not assess milk supply, diagnose feeding difficulty or replace hands-on lactation support. It gives you and your chosen professional clearer records to work from.

Mixed feeding without a sense of failure

Combination feeding can be planned, temporary, occasional or simply what works today. The app treats breast milk and formula as records in the same feeding history, not as opposing teams.

With Premium, FlowCast learns from the logged rhythm and, after about four days, suggests a likely window for the next feed. It is based on your baby's history and remains a guide, not a schedule you need to follow.

Useful history for IBCLCs and lactation experts

A consultation is more productive when nobody has to reconstruct a week from memory. Parents can bring feeding times, bottle amounts, pumping history, health notes and growth records into the conversation. Premium reports provide a shareable summary.

  • Review the sequence of feeds rather than isolated snapshots.
  • See bottle and formula details alongside nursing history.
  • Keep medicines, reactions and health notes available when relevant.
  • Use the record as a conversation aid, never as a diagnosis.

Feed Flow does not replace an IBCLC, midwife, nurse or physician, and it does not claim WHO Code certification or clinical endorsement.

Common questions

Can I breastfeed and formula feed in the same app?
Yes. Mixed feeding is a core part of Feed Flow. Nursing, pumping, expressed milk and formula stay in one timeline and daily overview.
Does Feed Flow assess milk supply?
No. It records feeding and pumping history but does not diagnose low supply, evaluate feeding quality or replace lactation support.
Can a lactation consultant use the history with me?
Yes. You can share your own records or a report during a consultation. The information is a conversation aid, not a clinical assessment.
Does FlowCast tell me when I must feed?
No. It estimates a likely window from your baby's logged rhythm. Your baby's cues and professional guidance come first.
