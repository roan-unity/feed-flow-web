Feed Flow and Huckleberry

Feed Flow or Huckleberry?

Huckleberry is a well-loved tracker known for sleep. Feed Flow does one thing: feeding.

Download on the App Store

Free to log · iPhone · Your baby’s data stays in your own iCloud

The core difference

  • Huckleberry is built around sleep (nap timing and sleep plans) and also tracks feeds, diapers and more, on iPhone and Android.
  • Feed Flow is built around feeding first, with health and growth tracking, on iPhone, and does not track sleep.

Huckleberry fits better if

  • sleep is the main challenge
  • you want sleep, diapers and feeds in one app
  • someone in the family uses Android

Feed Flow fits better if

  • feeding is the hard part, especially formula, bottles or combo feeding
  • you want every prepared bottle to count down on your Lock Screen
  • you want free sharing, no ads and data in your own iCloud

What Feed Flow is, and what it is not

Feed Flow is an iPhone app built around feeding first: bottles of formula or expressed milk, breastfeeding with a timer and sides, pumping, water and solids in one timeline. It is made in Norway by a dad who combo fed and kept losing track of bottles.

  • Free: logging every feed, a countdown on every prepared bottle that stays on your Lock Screen, sharing with your partner or other caregivers, the nine-allergen journey with reaction notes, and a vitamin D tracker that also counts the vitamin D in formula. No ads.
  • Premium (monthly, annual or lifetime): WHO growth curves, a PDF report for your pediatrician, the next-feed forecast, deeper analytics, unlimited history and more than one baby.
  • Private: the log lives on your phone and in your own iCloud. We never see it.
  • Not included: sleep tracking, diaper tracking, and apps for Android or Apple Watch.

Common questions

Is Feed Flow a Huckleberry alternative?
For feeding, it can be. Feed Flow focuses only on feeding. It does not track sleep, so if sleep is why you use Huckleberry, keep Huckleberry.
Can I use Feed Flow and Huckleberry together?
Yes. Some parents use Huckleberry for sleep and Feed Flow for bottles and feeding.
