Choose Huckleberry if

  • sleep is the main challenge and you want nap-time predictions or a sleep plan
  • you want sleep, diapers and feeds in one app
  • someone in the family uses Android

Choose Feed Flow if

  • feeding is the hard part, especially formula, bottles or combo feeding
  • you want every prepared bottle to count down on your Lock Screen so nothing is forgotten in the fridge or on the counter
  • you want free sharing, no ads and data that stays in your own iCloud

What Feed Flow is (and is not)

Feed Flow is an iPhone app (iOS 18 or later) built only around feeding: bottles of formula or expressed milk, breastfeeding with a timer and sides, pumping, water and solids in one timeline. It is made by a small team in Norway, started by a dad who combo fed and kept losing track of bottles.

  • Free, forever: logging every feed, the bottle countdown on the Lock Screen, family sharing with your partner or other caregivers, the nine-allergen journey with reaction notes, and the vitamin D tracker that also counts the vitamin D in formula.
  • Premium (monthly, annual with a 14-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase): WHO growth curves, a PDF report for your pediatrician, the next-feed forecast (FlowCast), deeper analytics, unlimited history and more than one baby.
  • No ads and no tracking. Your log lives on your phone and in your own iCloud; we never see it.
  • What it does not do: sleep tracking, diaper tracking, an Android app or an Apple Watch app. It is also a new app with only a handful of App Store ratings so far.

Common questions

Is Feed Flow a Huckleberry alternative?
For feeding, yes. Feed Flow focuses only on feeding, with a free Lock Screen bottle countdown and vitamin D tracking. It does not track sleep, so if sleep timing is why you use Huckleberry, keep Huckleberry.
Can I use Feed Flow and Huckleberry together?
Yes. Some parents use Huckleberry for sleep and Feed Flow for bottles and feeding.
