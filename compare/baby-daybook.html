Feed Flow and Baby Daybook

Feed Flow or Baby Daybook?

Baby Daybook is a very configurable all-in-one tracker. Feed Flow keeps feeding simple.

Download on the App Store

Free to log · iPhone · Your baby’s data stays in your own iCloud

The core difference

  • Baby Daybook tracks feeds, sleep, diapers, health and custom activities, on iPhone and Android.
  • Feed Flow is built only around feeding, on iPhone, with fewer settings on purpose.

Baby Daybook fits better if

  • you want to customize every activity you log
  • you want sleep and diapers too
  • you need Android

Feed Flow fits better if

  • you want a calm feeding-only view
  • you want a free bottle countdown and vitamin D from formula counted for you

What Feed Flow is, and what it is not

Feed Flow is an iPhone app built only around feeding: bottles of formula or expressed milk, breastfeeding with a timer and sides, pumping, water and solids in one timeline. It is made in Norway by a dad who combo fed and kept losing track of bottles.

  • Free: logging every feed, a countdown on every prepared bottle that stays on your Lock Screen, sharing with your partner or other caregivers, the nine-allergen journey with reaction notes, and a vitamin D tracker that also counts the vitamin D in formula. No ads.
  • Premium (monthly, annual or lifetime): WHO growth curves, a PDF report for your pediatrician, the next-feed forecast, deeper analytics, unlimited history and more than one baby.
  • Private: the log lives on your phone and in your own iCloud. We never see it.
  • Not included: sleep tracking, diaper tracking, and apps for Android or Apple Watch.
