Good to know

Straight answers.

Is it really free, forever?
Yes. Your code unlocks Feed Flow! Lifetime, the same thing other families buy once in the app. No card, no trial that runs out, nothing to cancel.
Which countries does the code work in?
A code only works in the country your App Store account is set to. Pick that country in the form and we send a code that works there: the US, the UK, Canada, Australia, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Mexico and Colombia. Somewhere else? Choose Another country and Robin will write to you. The app is free to download everywhere, and logging feeds and sharing with a partner are free.
Do I have to post or review anything?
No. The code is yours with nothing attached. What helps us most is that you use the app for real and tell us what is confusing or missing, and that is up to you.
What happens with my email?
We use it to send your code and one check-in email a few days later. No newsletter, and we never share or sell it. Write to support@tryfeedflow.app and we delete it.
What do I need?
An iPhone and an App Store account. Feed Flow! is an iPhone app.
What if the 100 spots are gone?
Then the form closes. The app stays free to download, with free feed logging and sharing.
