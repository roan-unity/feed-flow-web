Shared baby not syncing between phones? How to fix it

Updated November 2026 · For Feed Flow on iPhone · Les på norsk

Your logs are not lost. Each phone keeps every feed it logged, and those feeds upload once the connection is sorted out.

When you share a baby in Feed Flow, the log lives in the iCloud of the person who added the baby and sent the invite. We call them the owner. Apple carries every feed between your phones, and we have no server in the middle. That keeps your family's data private. It also means some of what decides whether sync works sits in iOS and iCloud, outside the app.

Go through the checks below in order. The most common causes come first.

  1. The 30-second check
  2. Is your partner logging on the shared baby?
  3. Was the invite accepted?
  4. iCloud on both phones
  5. Updates arrive late, or only when the app is open
  6. Force a refresh
  7. What is not shared yet
  8. Still stuck?

1. The 30-second check

  • Update Feed Flow on both phones. Open the App Store, tap your picture in the top corner, and install the Feed Flow update if one is listed. Sharing works best when both phones run the same version.
  • Open Feed Flow on both phones and leave it on screen for a moment. Updates arrive fastest while the app is open.

Now log a test feed on one phone and look at the other. If it shows up, you are done, and you can delete the test feed.

2. Is your partner logging on the shared baby?

This is the most common cause. When your partner opened the invite, Feed Flow showed a screen called You’re in! with two buttons: Start with (baby’s name) and Maybe later. If they tapped Maybe later, or had already added the baby themselves before the invite came, their phone now has two babies with the same name. Their feeds go to their own copy, and nobody else can see it.

Look at
Your partner’s phone. Open Family in the bottom bar and find the section Shared with you.
It should say
A row with the baby’s name that reads Shared by (your name) · you can log & view.
What to do
Tap that row to switch to the shared baby. From then on, their feeds go into the shared log.
The baby’s name at the top of the Today screen shows which baby is selected. Tap the name to see the list and check that the shared baby is the one picked.
No Shared with you section at all? Then the invite has not been accepted yet. Go to step 3.

Feeds your partner logged on their own copy stay on that copy. If you want the whole day in one place, they can add those feeds again on the shared baby.

3. Was the invite accepted?

Look at
The owner’s phone. Open Family and find the baby under Your children.
It should say
You + 1 caregiver under the baby’s name, once your partner has joined and logged. With two helpers it says You + 2 caregivers, and so on.
What to do
If you see Send a new invite, tap it and send the invite again. An app update gave each baby its own private space in iCloud, and invites sent before that update stopped working. Your logs were not touched. Only the invite needs sending again.

When your partner opens the new invite, three things matter:

  • They open it on their iPhone, with Feed Flow already installed.
  • Their iPhone is signed in to the same Apple ID the invite was sent to. If you sent it to a phone number or an email address, that number or address has to belong to the Apple ID on their phone.
  • If they see Couldn’t join the shared log, the invite was most likely opened under a different Apple ID. Send a new invite to the phone number or email of the Apple ID their iPhone is signed in with.

4. iCloud on both phones In iOS settings

This part is set by iOS, not by Feed Flow, so check it on both phones.

Look at
The iPhone’s Settings app. Tap your name at the top, then iCloud.
It should say
You are signed in. Under Saved to iCloud, tap See All: Feed Flow! is switched on. iCloud Drive is on too.
What to do
Sign in, or switch on Feed Flow! in that list. Then open Feed Flow again.

Check the owner’s iCloud storage. The shared log is stored in the owner’s iCloud. When that storage is full, new feeds stop saving to iCloud for everyone who shares the baby, not just the owner. Free up space or add storage on the owner’s account, and the waiting feeds upload.

In the app: if Family shows Sign in to iCloud to sync under Sync now, that phone is signed out of iCloud.

5. Updates arrive late, or only when the app is open Set by iOS

iOS decides when an app may sync in the background, and it holds updates back to save battery. So while Feed Flow is closed, a feed can take a while to reach the other phone. With the app open, updates usually arrive within seconds.

These slow background updates down:

  • Low Power Mode is on. The battery icon turns yellow.
  • Background App Refresh is off. Check Settings → General → Background App Refresh: the setting at the top should not say Off, and Feed Flow! should be switched on in the list.
  • Feed Flow was swiped away in the app switcher. iOS then waits until you open the app again. Leaving it in the app switcher is fine.

Feed alerts are sent when the phone syncs in the background, so an alert can land a little after the feed itself. You can check them in the Feed alerts row in Family, and in Settings → Notifications → Feed Flow!.

6. Force a refresh

  • In Family, tap Sync now.
  • Or open Settings → Data & privacy. Under iCloud, tap Sync now or Re-download from iCloud. Sync now asks iCloud for what changed. Re-download asks for everything, and it only adds, it never deletes.

What the message under Re-download means:

  • Nothing new. This device already has everything in your iCloud. This phone is up to date with iCloud. If feeds are still missing, they have not reached iCloud from the other phone yet. Go through steps 4 and 5 on that phone.
  • A message that starts with No history arrived for (name), a baby shared with you: ask the owner to open Feed Flow and send a new invite from Family.

Then close the app fully and open it again: swipe up from the bottom of the screen, swipe Feed Flow away, and tap its icon. Opening it fresh runs the iCloud setup again.

7. What is not shared yet

Some things stay on the phone that logged them in the current version. If one of these is missing on the other phone, nothing is broken.

  • Shared between phones today: bottles, nursing and solids, growth measurements, medicines, vaccines and the baby’s details.
  • Stays on the phone that logged it today: reactions, sick days, pumping sessions, formula brand periods, milestones, saved solids presets, and supplements such as vitamin D drops.

We will update this page when that changes.

8. Still stuck?

Write to us at support@tryfeedflow.app. We are a small team and we read every message. It helps a lot if you send:

  • a screenshot of the Family screen on both phones,
  • the message you get after tapping Re-download from iCloud,
  • which iPhone and iOS version each phone has (Settings → General → About).

An upcoming update adds a troubleshooting screen under Settings → Help, with a button that copies sync diagnostics for you to paste into the email. Until it arrives, the three things above tell us what we need.
