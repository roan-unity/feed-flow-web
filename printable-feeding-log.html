Free printable newborn feeding log

Download a one-page feeding log for the first weeks: twelve rows per day for time, breastfeeding minutes on each side, when a bottle was made, formula or breast milk, amount offered and drunk, pumping, and wet and dirty diapers. Free, no sign-up, in US Letter and A4.

Download the feeding log

One page covers one day. Print a stack, keep it on the fridge or by the bed, and start a new sheet each morning.

Free to print and share with your partner, family or childcare. Please link to this page rather than re-hosting the file.

What each column is for

  • Time: when the feed started.
  • Breast, L / R min: minutes on each side, if you nurse.
  • Bottle made at: the time you prepared the bottle, so anyone can see how long it has been out.
  • Type: circle F for formula or BM for breast milk.
  • Offered and drank: what went in the bottle and what your baby actually took, in ml or oz.
  • Pumped: how much you expressed.
  • Wet and dirty: tick the diapers.
  • Day totals: add up feeds, amounts and diapers at the end of the day, which is often what a pediatrician or health nurse asks about.

How often do newborns feed?

Breastfed newborns usually feed about 8 to 12 times in 24 hours, according to the CDC. Formula-fed babies often feed a little less often. Every baby is different, so twelve rows is room to write, not a target. Follow your baby's hunger cues and your pediatrician's advice.

A record, not medical advice

If you are worried about how your baby is feeding, the number of wet diapers, or how your baby seems, contact your pediatrician, midwife or health nurse. Prepared bottles: follow the formula label and your local guidance on how long a bottle can stay out.

When paper gets hard to share

Paper works well until two people are feeding the baby, or the sheet is in the kitchen when you are in the nursery. Feed Flow keeps the same record on every caregiver's iPhone, counts the day's totals for you and shows a countdown on the Lock Screen from when each bottle was made, as a memory aid. Logging is free, with no ads.

Common questions

Is the printable feeding log free?
Yes. Download it as a PDF in US Letter or A4, with no sign-up.
How many feeds fit on one page?
Twelve rows per page, one page per day, with a totals row for feeds, amounts and diapers.
Does the log work for both breastfeeding and formula?
Yes. It has columns for minutes on each side, bottle type and amounts, and pumping, so it works for nursing, bottle feeding and combo feeding.
Is it available in other languages?
Yes. The same log is available in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, German, Spanish, Finnish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese.
