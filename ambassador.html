Before you apply

Good to know.

Can I earn money as a mom influencer?
Yes, once you are accepted as an ambassador. You earn 35–50% commission on net profit from app sales through your own link or code. We settle your exact rate and the practical details together before you start. What you earn depends on how many parents buy, so we cannot promise an amount.
Do I need a big following?
Not at all. There is no follower minimum. A small account that parents trust is exactly what we are looking for, and we read every application one by one.
Can IBCLCs and lactation experts apply?
Yes, where your professional rules and your employer allow it. Any recommendation has to be genuine, optional for the family and disclosed.
Is there commission for recruiting other ambassadors?
No. It is one level only. You earn on app sales through your own link or code, and nobody is asked to recruit anyone.
