Before you apply

Clear answers, no hidden structure.

Can I earn money as a mom influencer?
Yes, if you are eligible and accepted. Parent creators can earn 35–50% commission on net profit from eligible app sales attributed to their own link or code. Rates, products, reporting and payout terms are confirmed before participation; earnings are not guaranteed.
Can IBCLCs and lactation experts apply?
Yes, where local rules, employer policies and professional obligations allow it. Recommendations must be genuine, optional and properly disclosed. Associations may need an organizational rather than personal arrangement.
Is there commission for recruiting other ambassadors?
No. Feed Flow uses a single-level model. There are no recruitment commissions, downlines or pyramid structures. Commission is only for eligible app sales directly attributed to you.
What does 35–50% of net profit mean?
It is a percentage of the net profit defined in your agreement, not the customer's full purchase price. App-store fees, taxes, refunds and other agreed adjustments affect the calculation. Your exact rate and eligible sales are confirmed before you start.
Do I have to publish a positive review?
No. Try the app and share only if you genuinely find it useful. Feed Flow does not require positive reviews, a particular rating or scripted claims.
