Who should pick which app

  • Sleep is your main worry: Huckleberry. Its sleep timing and coaching are what it is known for, and Feed Flow does not track sleep.
  • You want one app for diapers, sleep, feeds and meds, with the largest user base: Baby Tracker by Nighp (about 227,000 ratings on the US App Store) or Baby Daybook.
  • You or a caregiver use Android: any of the others. Feed Flow is iPhone only.
  • Feeding is the hard part, especially formula, bottles or combo feeding: Feed Flow. Every bottle gets a countdown on your Lock Screen from the moment you make it, the app learns the amounts your baby usually takes so you pour closer to what they drink, and it adds up vitamin D from drops and formula. All of that is free, with no ads.

What Feed Flow is (and is not)

Feed Flow is an iPhone app (iOS 18 or later) built only around feeding: bottles of formula or expressed milk, breastfeeding with a timer and sides, pumping, water and solids in one timeline. It is made by a small team in Norway, started by a dad who combo fed and kept losing track of bottles.

  • Free, forever: logging every feed, the bottle countdown on the Lock Screen, family sharing with your partner or other caregivers, the nine-allergen journey with reaction notes, and the vitamin D tracker that also counts the vitamin D in formula.
  • Premium (monthly, annual with a 14-day free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase): WHO growth curves, a PDF report for your pediatrician, the next-feed forecast (FlowCast), deeper analytics, unlimited history and more than one baby.
  • No ads and no tracking. Your log lives on your phone and in your own iCloud; we never see it.
  • What it does not do: sleep tracking, diaper tracking, an Android app or an Apple Watch app. It is also a new app with only a handful of App Store ratings so far.

Common questions

What is the best baby feeding tracker app for iPhone?
It depends on what you need most. For sleep, Huckleberry. For logging everything with the biggest user base, Baby Tracker by Nighp. If feeding itself is the hard part (formula, bottles, pumping or combo feeding), Feed Flow is built only around that, with a free Lock Screen bottle countdown, free sharing and no ads.
Which app is best for combo feeding (breast and formula)?
Most trackers log both. Feed Flow keeps breastfeeding, pumping, expressed milk and formula in one timeline and one milk total, with a countdown on every prepared bottle. It is iPhone only.
Is there a free baby feeding tracker without ads?
Feed Flow is free to log, with no ads and no tracking. Baby Tracker by Nighp is free with ads and sells a one-time ad removal.
Which baby tracker apps work on Android?
Huckleberry, Baby Tracker (Nighp), Nara, Baby Daybook and Glow all have Android apps. Feed Flow is iPhone only.
