Choosing a baby tracker

Which baby tracker fits your family?

Most baby trackers log the same basics. The real difference is what each app is built around. Here is an honest guide, including when another app is the better choice.

Download on the App Store

Free to log · iPhone · Your baby’s data stays in your own iCloud

The core differences

  • Huckleberry is built around sleep, with nap timing and sleep plans, and tracks feeds too. It has an Android app.
  • Baby Tracker by Nighp, Nara Baby and Baby Daybook are all-in-one trackers for feeds, sleep, diapers and more, and have Android apps.
  • Feed Flow is built only around feeding, on iPhone. It does not track sleep or diapers.

Who should pick which

  • Sleep is your main worry: Huckleberry.
  • You want feeds, sleep and diapers in one app: one of the all-in-one trackers.
  • Someone in the family uses Android: any of the others. Feed Flow is iPhone only.
  • Feeding is the hard part, especially formula, bottles, pumping or combo feeding: Feed Flow. Every prepared bottle counts down on your Lock Screen, the app learns the amounts your baby usually takes so you pour closer to what they drink, and it adds up vitamin D from drops and formula. Free, with no ads.

What Feed Flow is, and what it is not

Feed Flow is an iPhone app built only around feeding: bottles of formula or expressed milk, breastfeeding with a timer and sides, pumping, water and solids in one timeline. It is made in Norway by a dad who combo fed and kept losing track of bottles.

  • Free: logging every feed, a countdown on every prepared bottle that stays on your Lock Screen, sharing with your partner or other caregivers, the nine-allergen journey with reaction notes, and a vitamin D tracker that also counts the vitamin D in formula. No ads.
  • Premium (monthly, annual or lifetime): WHO growth curves, a PDF report for your pediatrician, the next-feed forecast, deeper analytics, unlimited history and more than one baby.
  • Private: the log lives on your phone and in your own iCloud. We never see it.
  • Not included: sleep tracking, diaper tracking, and apps for Android or Apple Watch.

Common questions

What is the best baby feeding tracker app for iPhone?
It depends on what you need most. For sleep, Huckleberry. For feeds, sleep and diapers in one app, an all-in-one tracker such as Baby Tracker, Nara or Baby Daybook. If feeding itself is the hard part (formula, bottles, pumping or combo feeding), Feed Flow is built only around that, with a free Lock Screen bottle countdown, free sharing and no ads.
Which app is good for combo feeding (breast and formula)?
Feed Flow keeps breastfeeding, pumping, expressed milk and formula in one timeline and one milk total, with a countdown on every prepared bottle. It is iPhone only.
Is there a free baby feeding tracker without ads?
Feed Flow is free to log, with no ads and no tracking. Premium adds growth curves, a pediatrician report and the next-feed forecast.
Does Feed Flow track sleep or diapers?
No. Feed Flow is built only around feeding. If you want sleep or diapers too, an all-in-one tracker or Huckleberry fits better, and some parents use one of those alongside Feed Flow.
