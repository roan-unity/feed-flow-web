Bottle countdown

Is there an app that tells you when a prepared bottle has to go?

Yes. Feed Flow starts a countdown the moment you make a bottle and keeps it on your iPhone Lock Screen. The default is one hour for formula and two hours for pumped milk, and you set it to match your label and local guidance. The countdown is free.

How the bottle countdown works

Log a bottle when you make it. Feed Flow starts counting from that moment, not from when the feed begins, so the clock reflects how long the milk has actually been out.

  • On the Lock Screen. The time left stays visible without unlocking the phone or opening the app, which is the point at 3am.
  • Formula and pumped milk. By default one hour for formula and two hours for pumped milk. Change either in Settings to follow your formula label or your local health authority.
  • Every caregiver sees it. When the bottle is in the shared baby log, whoever picks it up next sees the same countdown.
  • Free. The countdown is part of free logging, with no ads.

A memory aid, not a safety sensor

The countdown measures time and nothing else. Feed Flow cannot know how a bottle was stored, whether it was warmed, how warm the room is or whether your baby has already drunk from it. Guidance also differs between countries and products.

The label and your health authority decide

Use the timer you set to remember, and the formula label or local guidance to decide. If you are unsure whether a bottle is still fine, make a fresh one.

Why we built it

Feed Flow started with a dad in Norway and a lot of poured-out bottles, because it was hard to remember when each one was made. A countdown that lives on the Lock Screen answers the one question you have when you find a half-full bottle on the counter: how long has this been out?

The rest of the bottle stays on record too: what was prepared, how much your baby took and what was left, in ml or oz.

Common questions

Does the countdown start when I make the bottle or when my baby starts drinking?
When you make it. Feed Flow counts from the time the bottle is prepared.
Can I change the countdown time?
Yes. The defaults are one hour for formula and two hours for pumped milk, and you can change both in Settings to follow your formula label or local guidance.
Is the bottle countdown free?
Yes. The Lock Screen countdown is part of Feed Flow's free logging, with no ads.
Does the timer tell me the bottle is safe?
No. It is a memory aid based on time, not a safety sensor. Follow the product label and your local health authority's guidance.
