Automatic vitamin D calculations from drops and formula

Feed Flow combines two sources in one daily readout: the supplement drops you log and the vitamin D carried by today's formula feeds. Formula is calculated from the feed amount and the vitamin D value read from the label. Known products are filled in from our catalogue, so check them against your tin.

  • Logged drops contribute the dose you recorded.
  • Formula contributes according to actual consumed amounts and your label's per-100 ml value.
  • The card separates formula and drops so the total has an understandable source.
  • If a formula has no label value entered, the app discloses that it is not counted rather than guessing.

Breast milk is not assigned an estimated vitamin D contribution. Feed Flow does not invent a fortification value for a brand.

See what remains toward the daily reference

The meter uses 10 µg / 400 IU per day as the reference for a baby's first year, the figure the American Academy of Pediatrics, Helsedirektoratet and ESPGHAN give. It can show the counted amount so far, what remains toward that reference, and when your planned drops will cover the rest.

This is arithmetic against a printed reference, not a judgement that your baby is deficient, adequately supplemented or receiving the right dose. Recommendations differ by feeding method, formula intake, age and country.

Your clinician sets the plan, not the app

Use the supplement label and advice from your pediatrician, pharmacist or child health service. Feed Flow does not prescribe a dose, assess a deficiency or check interactions.

A daily log and optional reminder that stays gentle

Mark today's drops with one tap and see when they were logged. An optional reminder helps you remember, while a simple streak can make the routine visible without turning a missed day into a failure.

Caregivers can see the same record through the shared baby log, reducing the chance that anyone has to ask whether the drops were already given.

Official guidance stays one step away

For an example of feeding-specific national advice, the UK NHS explains vitamin supplements for babies and children. The US CDC provides separate guidance for breast milk, mixed feeding and infant formula.

Those sources illustrate why a universal automatic dose would be inappropriate. Use the recommendation for your country and your child.

Common questions

Does Feed Flow calculate vitamin D from formula?
Yes. It uses consumed formula amounts and the vitamin D value on the formula label. It never guesses a missing brand value.
Can it show how much is left to supplement?
It shows what remains toward the 10 µg / 400 IU daily reference and when your planned drops cover the rest. That is reference arithmetic, not a personalized dosing instruction.
Does it remind me to give vitamin D drops?
Yes. You can log daily drops and enable an optional reminder. A missed day simply starts a new streak without scolding.
Does the app decide whether my baby needs a supplement?
No. Your child's clinician, pharmacist, product label and national guidance determine the appropriate plan. Feed Flow keeps records and performs calculations.
