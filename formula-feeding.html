A formula feeding tracker built around real bottles

A bottle is more than a number in a daily total. You may want to remember which formula you used, when it was prepared, when feeding started, how much your baby took and how much was left. Feed Flow keeps those details together so the next caregiver does not have to reconstruct the story.

  • Record prepared and consumed amounts in ml or oz.
  • See formula and expressed-milk bottles alongside nursing and pumping.
  • Keep a history of the formula or brand used and your baby's response.
  • Use your baby's usual sizes as a practical guide when preparing the next bottle.

A little milk left in a bottle is normal. Feed Flow is designed to reduce avoidable waste, not to pressure a baby to finish or make a parent feel guilty.

A bottle countdown that stays on your lock screen

Start a prepared bottle in the app and its countdown starts with it. The remaining time stays visible on your iPhone lock screen, so you do not need to unlock the phone and search through an app at 3am.

The countdown is a memory aid, not a sensor. Feed Flow cannot know where a bottle was stored, whether it was touched or heated, or whether local safety guidance differs. Set and interpret timers using the formula label and guidance from your local health authority.

Compare formula brands using their actual labels

Searching for a formula brand often leads to conflicting marketing claims. Feed Flow's formula finder gives you a record-keeping alternative: compare the products you use side by side using the nutrition information printed on their labels.

  • Keep formula brand and product details with feeding history.
  • Compare label-listed energy, protein, fat and carbohydrates.
  • Record vitamin D from formula alongside separate supplement drops.
  • Bring a clear history to a pediatrician, dietitian or feeding consultation.

Feed Flow does not rank brands, recommend a switch or decide which formula is suitable for your baby. Those decisions belong with you and a qualified professional who knows your child.

Smart meal planning and less leftover formula

FlowCast learns from your baby's own logged feeding rhythm. After about four days of history, it can show a likely next-feed time; Premium adds a day-ahead view. Usual bottle sizes help you prepare closer to what your baby typically takes.

This is not a schedule to enforce and it is not a medical prediction. It is a calm answer to a practical question: roughly how much should we get ready, and when might the next feed happen?

Common questions

Can I track different formula brands in Feed Flow?
Yes. You can keep formula product details with feeding records and compare label-listed nutrition. Feed Flow does not recommend or rank brands.
Does the bottle timer guarantee that formula is safe?
No. It is a countdown based on time, not a safety sensor. Follow the product label and local health guidance for preparation, storage and disposal.
Can Feed Flow include vitamin D from formula?
Yes. Record the vitamin D information from the formula label and log separate vitamin D drops. The app keeps records; it does not recommend supplement doses.
Is mixed formula and breastfeeding supported?
Yes. Nursing, pumping, expressed milk and formula appear in the same feeding picture without judgement.
