Open to mom creators · Paid on commission

A way to earn as a mom creator, with an app you use.

Who we are

Feed Flow is an iPhone app for feeding, growth and baby health. A dad built it during his own night feeds. Parents log bottles, nursing, pumping and solids with one hand. Weight, allergens, medicines and vitamin D drops sit in the same record.

The idea behind it is simple. A tired parent should have less to remember. So the app is calm, it has no ads and no sponsored formula brands, and a baby's records stay on the parent's phone and in the family's own iCloud.

Parents trust another parent more than they trust an ad. That is why we are asking you.

See what the app does

What the work is

Use the app with your own baby first. If it earns a place in your day, show it the way you show anything else you like.

  • A screen recording while you log a night feed.
  • A story about the bottle you forgot you made, and how you check now.
  • An answer when someone asks how you keep track of feeds.

It is your account and your voice. There is no script, no posting schedule and no verdict you have to reach. If you do not like the app, do not post about it.

How the pay works

This is commission. It is not a salary and it is not a fee per post.

Eligible ambassadors earn 35–50% commission on net profit from app sales attributed to their own link or code. Net profit is what is left of a sale after app store fees, taxes, refunds and other agreed adjustments. We agree your exact rate and the payout terms with you before you start.

It is one level only. You earn on sales from your own link or code. There is no commission for recruiting other people and no downline.

We cannot tell you what you will earn. It depends on how many of your followers buy, and nobody knows that ahead of time. Being accepted is not guaranteed either.

Who fits

  • Moms and dads who post about everyday life with a baby.
  • Lifestyle creators whose followers are new or expecting parents.
  • UGC creators who make honest videos about things they use.
  • IBCLCs, lactation consultants and feeding professionals, where their professional rules allow it.

Small accounts are welcome. We look at trust before follower count, and we read applications one by one. Feed Flow is an iPhone app, so it fits best when your followers use iPhones.

What we ask of you

Two things. Tell your audience you are a Feed Flow ambassador whenever you share the app. And only say what you really think about it.

How to apply

The application is your name, your email and one link to where we can see your work. You can add a note if you want to. We do not ask for follower counts. We read every application ourselves and answer by email.

Common questions

Can I make money as a mom influencer with a small account?
You can apply with a small account. We do not set a follower minimum, and we look at trust and fit before numbers. What you earn depends on how many people buy through your link or code, so we cannot promise an amount.
Is this a job?
No. It is a commission program. You are not employed by Feed Flow and there is no salary or fee per post. You earn commission on app sales attributed to your own link or code.
How much is the commission?
Eligible ambassadors earn 35–50% commission on net profit from app sales attributed to their own link or code. We agree your exact rate and payout terms with you before you start.
Do I have to post a set number of times, or say something positive?
No. There is no script and no posting schedule. Share Feed Flow only if you like it, and say what you really think.
Do I have to recruit other creators?
No. It is one level only. There is no commission for recruiting anyone and no downline.
Does Feed Flow work on Android?
No. Feed Flow is an iPhone app, so it fits best when the parents who follow you use iPhones.

The role in short

  • Share an app you use, in your own words.
  • 35–50% commission on net profit from your own link or code.
  • Rate and payout terms agreed before you start.
  • One level. No recruiting.
  • Nothing is guaranteed.
